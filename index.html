Jusior

A Proposed Educational Level for Early High-School Graduation

Yves Lafortune
Published October 3, 2026 · Version 1

Definition

Jusior (noun)

A high-school student who, while in the junior year, completes the academic requirements normally associated with the senior year and becomes eligible to graduate one year ahead of the traditional graduation schedule.

About Jusior

Jusior is a proposed educational term describing a student who reaches senior-level academic completion while still classified as a junior, making early graduation possible.

The term is intended to provide a concise way to identify and discuss this educational pathway.

Purpose

This publication formally introduces the term Jusior as a proposed educational concept for discussion, documentation, and potential future use.
